- **Game modes:** time trial, or a race against AI opponents.
- **Vehicle physics** with the Chaos Vehicles plugin.
- **Procedurally generated cyberpunk city** built with CyberScape Pro.
- **Garage** for customising the car's materials, with saving and loading.
- **AI opponents** that follow splines for pathfinding.
