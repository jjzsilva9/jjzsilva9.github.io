I'm a Research Assistant in the [Rainbow Group](https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/research/rainbow/) at the University of Cambridge, supervised by [Rafał Mantiuk](https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/~rkm38/), working on **quality metrics for stereoscopic video**. More broadly, I'm interested in computer graphics, animation, image and video processing, and AI.

I hold an MSc in Computer Science (Augmented and Virtual Reality) with Distinction from [Trinity College Dublin](https://www.tcd.ie/), where I worked on real-time rendering, including [voxel cone tracing for global illumination](/projects/voxel-cone-tracing/), as well as animation, extended reality and video engineering. Before that I graduated with First Class Honours in Computer Science from the [University of York](https://www.york.ac.uk/), receiving the BCS award for the second-highest average mark in my cohort. My final-year project, carried out with AMD, predicted player viewpoint motion to enable speculative rendering in cloud gaming. It was published at the 2025 AMD Global Technical Authors Conference, where it won second place in its category.

In industry, I've worked on tools for Dolby Vision 2 as an image processing intern at Dolby Laboratories, and spent two summers as a software engineering intern at Microsoft in Azure.

Take a look at my [projects](/projects/), [publications](/publications/) and [CV](/cv/), or get in touch by email.
